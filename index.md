# Privacy Policy for BrainBoost

**Last updated:** November 8, 2026

**BrainBoost** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BrainBoost mobile application on Android and iOS.

---

### 1. Information We Store
- **On-device data only**: Your game scores, reaction times, accuracy, streaks, training history, cognitive benchmarks, badges, titles, Brain Sparks and theme preferences are stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.
- **No account required**: BrainBoost does not ask you to create an account or provide personal details such as your name, email address or phone number.
- **No server of our own**: We do not operate servers that receive your gameplay data.
- **Device backups**: If backups are enabled on your device, Android may include BrainBoost's data in your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This is controlled by you in your device settings.

---

### 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
BrainBoost is free and shows ads using the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)**.

- **What Google may collect**: advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA), IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google uses this to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.
- **Consent**: In regions where it is required (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from **Settings > Ad privacy choices**.
- **iOS tracking**: On iOS, the app asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using that identifier.
- **Your controls**: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Android: *Settings > Google > Ads*; iOS: *Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking*).
- **More information**: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

---

### 3. In-App Purchases
BrainBoost offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see or store your payment details. The app only records on your device that the purchase was made, and asks the store to confirm it so it can be restored after a reinstall. Refer to [Google Play's](https://policies.google.com/privacy) or [Apple's](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) privacy policy for how they process payments.

---

### 4. Device Permissions
- **Internet and network state**: Used to load ads and open links.
- **Notifications**: Used only for local daily workout reminders that you turn on in Settings. No notifications are sent from external servers.
- **Vibration**: Used for haptic feedback during games. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Run at startup**: Used to restore your scheduled reminders after your device restarts.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
BrainBoost is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

### 6. Data Export and Deletion
- You can export your progress as a JSON file from **Settings**.
- You can permanently erase all app data with **Settings > Reset All Data**, by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.
- For data collected by Google for advertising, use the controls described in section 2.

---

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
